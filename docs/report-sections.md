# Report Sections

TrainLens reports are generated by the configured LLM from structured notebook
context. They are designed to explain the result of a training run, not just
print metric values.

## Training Summary

This section lists the dataset, model context, training parameters, and other
high-level facts found in the notebook state.

## Result Explanation

This section interprets the result from the evidence provided in the notebook,
for example whether metrics suggest improvement, plateauing, or overfitting.

## Metrics And Trace

Metric and trace evidence connect values to concrete events such as batches,
eval steps, epochs, and checkpoints when that information exists in memory.

## Potential Issues

Risks and caveats should stay grounded in the notebook evidence. The LLM is
prompted to avoid unsupported claims when context is missing.

## Recommended Next Steps

Recommendations are specific actions with rationale. They should be small
enough to try in the next experiment.

## Improvement Plan

The bottom line summarizes the most important diagnosis and the next action to
try.
